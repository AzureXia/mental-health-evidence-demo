Review the evidence and the graph design

Start with these four examples, then add comments in the review CSV.

  • Biofeedback: are the wait-list and active-treatment comparisons kept separate?
  • Motherly: does the graph preserve a nonsignificant comparison without turning it into a claim of no benefit?
  • C3: are inverse correlations distinct from associations that were not detected?
  • SSRIs after stroke: are benefits, harms, population and drug-specific limits preserved?

For each row, check the relation against the quote, its context and the full stored passage. Also flag important findings that are missing from these passages. In statements.csv, use reviewer_decision (accept, revise, reject or unsure) and reviewer_notes. State the correction and quote that supports it.

For graph integration, identify the target entity ID, any predicate changes, and one query you need this evidence layer to support. Population and comparator information is currently kept in source context and notes, not normalized query fields. The page does not collect or save feedback.
